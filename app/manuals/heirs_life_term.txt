Term Life Insurance Underwriting Manual
Organization: Heirs Life Assurance
Product ID: life_term_001

1. Eligibility Rules
   - Proposer Age: 18 to 60 years at entry.
   - Max Coverage Age: 65 years.
   - Residency: Must be a resident of Nigeria.
   - BMI: Must be between 18.5 and 30.
     * If BMI > 30: Load premium by 25%.
     * If BMI > 35: Require medical exam.

2. Premium Calculation Rules
   - Base Premium: (Sum Assured * Rate) / 1000.
   - Rates per 1000 Sum Assured (by Age Band):
     * 18-30: 2.5
     * 31-40: 3.5
     * 41-50: 6.0
     * 51-60: 12.0
   - Smoker Loading: Add 50% to premium if smoker.
   - Occupation Loading:
     * High Risk (Mining, Pilot, construction): Add 30% loading.
     * Standard (Office): No loading.

3. Medical Underwriting Limits (Non-Medical Limits)
   - Up to N10,000,000 Sum Assured: No medicals required (Health Declaration only).
   - N10m - N50m: Tele-underwriting required.
   - Above N50m: Full medicals required.

4. Exclusions
   - Suicide within first 12 months.
   - Pre-existing conditions not disclosed.
   - Death resulting from participation in criminal acts.
