Gadget Protection Plan Underwriting Manual
Organization: Heirs Gadget Insurance
Product ID: gadget_protect_001

1. Eligible Devices
   - Smartphones (Apple, Samsung, Google, Tecno, Infinix).
   - Laptops (MacBook, Dell, HP, Lenovo).
   - Tablets (iPad, Samsung Tab).

2. Eligibility Rules
   - Device Age: Must be less than 24 months old at purchase of policy.
   - Proof of Purchase: Original receipt required.
   - Condition: Screen must be intact (no cracks) at inception.
     * Remote verification via screen mirror test required.

3. Premium Calculation Rules
   - Screen Damage Only: 3% of Device Value.
   - Full Cover (Theft + Damage + Liquid): 6% of Device Value.
   - Deductible/Excess: 10% of claim value.

4. Coverage Details
   - Accidental Screen Damage: Replacement of screen.
   - Liquid Damage: Repair or replacement.
   - Theft: Replacement (Police report required).
   - Max Claims: 2 claims per policy year.

5. Exclusions
   - Cosmetic damage (scratches/dents) that doesn't affect functionality.
   - Theft from unattended vehicles.
   - Loss of data.
   - Accessories (chargers, headphones).
