Comprehensive Auto Insurance Underwriting Manual
Organization: Heirs General Insurance
Product ID: auto_comprehensive_001

1. Eligibility Rules
   - Vehicle Age: Must be less than 15 years old.
     * If vehicle_age > 15: Decline (Refer to Third Party Only).
   - Driver Age: Must be between 18 and 70 years.
     * If age < 18: Decline.
     * If age > 70: Refer to supervisors.
   - Vehicle Usage: Private or Commercial.
   - Vehicle Value: Minimum N1,000,000.

2. Premium Calculation Rules
   - Base Rate: 3% of Vehicle Value.
   - Age Loading:
     * If driver_age < 25: Add 10% loading.
     * If driver_age > 60: Add 5% loading.
   - No Claim Discount (NCD):
     * 1 year claim-free: 5% discount.
     * 2 years: 10% discount.
     * 3+ years: 15% discount.
   - Usage Loading:
     * Commercial usage: Add 20% to base rate.

3. Coverage Benefits
   - Own Damage: Covered up to 100% of value.
   - Third Party Property Damage: Limit N1,000,000.
   - Third Party Bodily Injury: Unlimited.
   - Medical Expenses: Limit N50,000 per occupant.

4. Exclusions
   - Driving under influence of alcohol/drugs.
   - Driving without a valid license.
   - Usage for racing or speed testing.
